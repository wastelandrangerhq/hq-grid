Mail will be scanned for radioactivity and, upon decontamination and/or clearance, read and filed for timely reply.

Wasteland forums:

Snake Squeezins
On September 15, 1998, I established a discussion group on the Internet named Snake Squeezins. This eGroup turned Yahoo! Group had its own private e-mailing list, private chat room, and had over 280 members as of April, 2002. In this forum, one could read and post messages both through the web site and also through one's e-mail account. It was the biggest gathering of Rangers this side of the atmosphere, and when Brian Fargo was sniffing out interest in Wasteland 2, he started posting there. Sadly in 2020, Yahoo! shut it all down. I do have an archive of Snake Squeezins somewhere. One day I may try to host that final backup.

  ⇒ http://games.groups.yahoo.com/group/wasteland/ (the old Snake Squeezins URL)

Tales From the Wasteland
I established another eGroup (turned Yahoo! Group) on February 9, 1999, to host the construction of stories set in the Wasteland genre. Most stories here were written by multiple people taking turns; the story segments are archived at the HQ-Grid's Reading Room. It never really took off but it was fun while it lasted, and it also was shut down in 2020.

  ⇒ http://groups.yahoo.com/group/wasteland-tales/ (the old Tales From the Wasteland URL)
